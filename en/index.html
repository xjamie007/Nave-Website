About Nave

Your partner for websites, apps and AI in Luxembourg

Nave is a digital agency based in Diekirch. We build websites, online shops, mobile apps and AI agents for small and medium-sized businesses all over Luxembourg, from the Éislek in the north to the Minett in the south.

With us you get everything from one team: advice, concept, design, development and support after launch. We speak Luxembourgish, German, French and English, work at a fixed price and guide you through the SME Package Digital, through which the state covers up to 70% of eligible costs.
