Our process

From idea to launch

Five steps, a fixed price and clear agreements.

01

First conversation

We listen and get to know your project. We bring our own ideas and possible directions to that first conversation.

02

Concept and design

Together we work out the direction, the design and the prototype.

03

Development

Then we build the actual solution. We keep you posted on how far along we are.

04

Testing and launch

We test everything on phone, tablet and desktop. Once it all fits, the solution goes live.

05

Ongoing work

We stay available after launch. We keep developing and improving the solution.
