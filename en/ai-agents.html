AI agents

AI that saves you time

An AI agent is a program that handles tasks on its own: answering customer questions, sorting emails, preparing quotes or pulling data out of documents. We build these AI solutions for businesses in Luxembourg.

We start from your daily work and look for the tasks that take the most time. That is where we use AI, test it with you and stay around when something needs adjusting. Your data stays under your control.

  • Chatbots and assistants for your website
  • Automatic emails, quotes and reports
  • Extracting data from invoices and documents
  • Connected to your software and calendars
  • With data protection (GDPR) in mind

Frequently asked questions

What is an AI agent?

A program that uses AI to complete a task on its own, for example answering questions, sorting emails or reading documents. It follows rules that you set.

Is it worth it for a small business?

Yes. Small businesses often save a lot of time because they have nobody for routine work. We start with one concrete task.

What happens to my data?

We choose solutions that respect data protection and show you where your data is processed. Sensitive data stays under your control.

Is there state funding for AI?

Yes, the SME Package AI: 70% for projects between €3,000 and €25,000 excl. VAT. We are happy to explain how it works.
