What is the SME Package AI?

The SME Package AI is a scheme of the Ministry of the Economy for small and medium-sized businesses. It helps bring AI into a company’s processes: first an assessment of where the business stands today, then an AI solution that fits its needs.

The state covers 70% of eligible costs for projects between €3,000 and €25,000 excl. VAT. The SME Package AI is a separate scheme alongside the SME Package Digital.

Who is eligible?

Business permit

The company holds a business permit from the Ministry of the Economy.

SME

The company meets the SME criteria (staff, turnover and balance sheet).

Based in Luxembourg

The registered office is in Luxembourg.

How it works

1

Pre-analysis

An appointment with the House of Entrepreneurship (Chamber of Commerce) or eHandwierk (Chamber of Skilled Trades and Crafts) to identify the key AI actions.

2

Package and quote

With the adviser you choose the package and the provider. The quote is between €3,000 and €25,000 excl. VAT.

3

Application

The adviser files the application; the Ministry of the Economy decides.

4

Implementation

The provider, for example Nave, implements the AI solution.

5

Assessment

A meeting reviews how satisfied you are and what you save.

6

Payment

You pay the final invoice, then the state pays you your 70%.

Frequently asked questions

How much do I get back?

70% of eligible costs. For a project of €25,000 excl. VAT, that would be €17,500.

Can I use both the SME Package AI and the SME Package Digital?

They are two separate schemes. All aid together counts towards the EU de minimis rule, though. It is best to clarify this during the pre-analysis.

When do I get the money?

After the project is finished: you pay the provider’s invoice and the state reimburses your 70%.

Where do I start?

With a pre-analysis appointment at the House of Entrepreneurship or eHandwierk. If you like, we talk through your idea with you beforehand.
