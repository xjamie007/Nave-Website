Frequently asked questions

How much does a website cost?

It depends on the scope: a company site with a few pages costs less than a shop with many products. After the first meeting you get a fixed price. With the SME Package Digital, the state covers up to 70%.

How long until my website is online?

A company website is often online within a few weeks; an online shop takes a little longer. You get a timeline from the start.

Can I edit my website myself afterwards?

Yes. We show you how to change texts and images. If you prefer, we take care of it for you.

Can you redo an old website?

Yes. We take over your content, improve it and make sure your site does not lose its place on Google.
